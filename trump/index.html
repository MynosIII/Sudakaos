    Method

    Approval average. Every public job-approval poll since January 20, 2025 (VoteHub open polling data and Wikipedia's polling tables). For each day we average the polls that ended in the previous 21 days, weighting by recency (7-day half-life) and sample size (square root, capped at 3,000), and dividing each pollster's weight by its number of polls in the window so daily trackers cannot dominate.

    Media. 24 US outlets by RSS, tagged Left, Center or Right using widely cited media-bias ratings, plus Google News searches. A story counts as Trump coverage when the headline or summary names him. Topics are keyword families; tone is computed per headline.

    Public voice. Top comments on the most relevant YouTube videos about Trump in the last two weeks. Sentiment and emotion use an English transformer model (BERTweet via pysentimiento) when available, otherwise VADER. Only short anonymised excerpts with a link to the original are shown.

    Truth Social. His own public posts and their engagement. Posting hours are shown in Eastern Time.

    Other sources. GDELT 2.0 for worldwide coverage, Google Trends and Wikipedia page views for attention, the Federal Register for signed documents, Polymarket for prediction markets, FRED for the economy and the Meta Ad Library for paid political ads.
